About the studio

A good poster should be understood in three seconds

I am Anmol. I design ads for shops and small brands because I got tired of seeing good products wrapped in tired, crowded posters. People scroll fast and walk past faster, so your offer has to land the moment they glance at it.

Every project starts the same way. You tell me what you sell and who you want to reach, and I send back a design with one clear message, a strong headline and your contact number where it cannot be missed.

Browse the work below. If a style catches your eye, send it on WhatsApp and I will make something like it for your business.

  • Everything done on WhatsApp
  • Files sized for phone and print
